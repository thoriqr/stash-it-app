A two-up grid, the closest thing here to an inventory. It reads as a set of places rather than a list of names — and it is the layout most at risk of feeling like a game rather than an app.

Watch for: whether the slot frame earns its ink. A border around every item separates them better than a hairline, but it also adds a second rectangle next to the thumbnail rectangle. On the Collections grid, check whether the icon plus the name in a 104 px cell feels useful or decorative.
