Pending URLs

A section inside the Inbox, not a fifth destination. A pending link is a URL the client holds and the backend has not confirmed saving — it is not a saved item, and it is not the saved-items history. The section is a count and a list of URLs, nothing more: no titles, no previews, no local metadata.

Load-bearing distinction: four of these five are known not saved and may say so. Only the last one is uncertain, and only it offers “check your Inbox”. Offline is a fifth, separate state (no error.code at all) and is not drawn as a 503.
Deliberately absent: an expiry warning, an overflow screen, a queue capacity, a discard action and a provenance label (shared versus pasted). The expiry notification form, the queue capacity and the discard-or-retain rule are all still open (Q1, Q2, Q3), and drawing them would require numbers and forms that do not exist yet. Manual retry is the v1 policy: no automatic retry, no retry loops.
