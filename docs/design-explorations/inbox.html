Density: the intake band costs roughly 120 px of a 844 px screen, which leaves about three and a half slots visible without scrolling. That is the trade the approved direction makes: a visible, usable intake area above a list that is already comfortable. The list itself is unchanged from the baseline — no metadata was removed to make room.
Open questions left visible: when the input validates, and what the clipboard does when it holds nothing (Q13) — no empty-clipboard state is drawn, because inventing one would pre-empt the decision.
