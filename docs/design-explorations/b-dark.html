A two-up grid, the closest thing here to an inventory. It reads as a set of places rather than a list of names — and it is the layout most at risk of feeling like a game rather than an app.

Watch for: whether the slots still separate from the canvas once the background is dark. The separation is carried by one step of surface value plus a single hairline, and the preview well is recessed rather than outlined. The accent is the app’s green re-valued for charcoal. It is the same 157° hue as the light theme’s #4C6B5F, one step up in lightness so it holds on a dark surface.
